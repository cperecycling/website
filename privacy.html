Privacy

Privacy Policy

This website is designed to collect only the information you choose to send when you contact CPER.

Information you provide

If you contact CPER by email or through a form on this site, you may provide your name, company, email address, location and information about equipment or services you are asking about.

How it is used

Information you send is used to respond to your request, evaluate pickup or service requirements, prepare quotes and communicate about the project.

Static forms

The forms included in this website package open your email application and do not transmit form data to a separate website database. If CPER later connects the forms to a third-party form or CRM service, this policy should be updated to describe that service.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to info@cperecycling.ca.

CPER is a registered Canadian non-profit helping organizations securely reuse, recycle and recover value from retired electronics.
