A better destination for retired technology

Protect your data. Help the community. Reduce waste.

CPER (Circular Processing Electronics Recycling) is a registered non-profit dedicated to providing sustainable electronics recycling solutions for businesses, institutions, government agencies, charities, and the public. By prioritizing reuse before recycling, we extend the life of electronic equipment through refurbishment and donation whenever possible, while responsibly recycling materials that have reached the end of their useful life.

Data-bearing devices are sanitized in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 guidelines using appropriate Clear, Purge or Destroy methods. Sanitization results are verified, with serialized reporting and certificates available upon request.

From one-time cleanouts to recurring multi-location programs, our process is practical, transparent and built around your operational needs.

Reuse before recycling

Every asset is directed to its highest and best use.

Working equipment is prioritized for refurbishment, component recovery or donation so technology can remain useful for as long as possible.

Equipment that cannot be reused is sent to certified downstream processors dedicated to electronics recycling, where specialized processes maximize material recovery while ensuring environmentally responsible treatment of complex electronic waste.
