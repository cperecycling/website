Pickup questions

Planning a collection

Do you serve locations outside the cities listed?

Often. Nearby and additional locations can be reviewed based on equipment type, project volume, timing and available pickup routes.

What services are available?

Depending on the project, services may include electronics recycling, ITAD, secure data destruction, serialized reporting and value recovery.

How do I confirm availability?

Send the pickup city, equipment types, approximate quantities and preferred timing. We will review the project and confirm the best next step.
