A practical destination for retired technology

Electronics recycling for equipment of nearly every size and function.

We accept a broad range of electronic equipment from businesses, institutions, government agencies, charities and the public. Equipment is evaluated for reuse, refurbishment, component recovery, secure data destruction or responsible downstream recycling.

Acceptance can depend on condition, quantity, location and any regulatory, contamination or transportation requirements.

We accept most electronics containing either a circuit board or a cable.

This includes complete devices, computer and server components, printed circuit boards, power and data cables, peripherals, accessories and many specialized commercial electronics.

Accepted equipment

Computers, IT equipment, electronics, circuit boards, cables and more.

The categories below describe common materials accepted for electronics recycling. Contact CPER when planning a large project, specialized equipment pickup or mixed electronic load.

01

Computers and IT Equipment

Computer recycling for business, institutional and commercial technology assets.

Desktop computers, laptops, workstations, servers, thin clients, computer towers, MacBooks, monitors, keyboards, mice, docking stations and external drives.

02

Mobile Devices

Responsible recycling and recovery for portable electronics and mobile technology.

Smartphones, cellphones, tablets, e-readers, smartwatches, mobile hotspots and handheld devices.

03

Networking and Telecom Equipment

Network equipment recycling for offices, telecom environments and IT infrastructure.

Routers, switches, modems, firewalls, wireless access points, telecom equipment, VoIP phones, PBX systems and network appliances.

04

Data-Centre Equipment

Data-centre decommissioning and recycling support for enterprise infrastructure.

Servers, storage arrays, SAN and NAS systems, server racks, power distribution units, backup equipment and uninterruptible power supplies.

05

Computer Components

Recovery and recycling for reusable and end-of-life computer parts.

Processors, RAM, hard drives, solid-state drives, motherboards, graphics cards, power supplies, circuit boards, cooling fans and expansion cards.

06

Office Electronics

Electronics recycling for common office, retail and document-handling equipment.

Printers, scanners, photocopiers, fax machines, shredders, label printers, barcode scanners, point-of-sale systems and payment terminals.

07

Audio and Video Equipment

Recycling for commercial displays, audiovisual systems and media equipment.

Televisions, projectors, digital signage displays, speakers, amplifiers, receivers, DVD and Blu-ray players, cameras and video equipment.

08

Medical and Laboratory Electronics

Specialized electronic equipment may be reviewed when regulatory and contamination requirements are satisfied.

Patient monitors, diagnostic equipment, optical and optometry equipment, laboratory instruments and electronic testing devices. Equipment must be properly decontaminated and may be subject to additional acceptance requirements.

09

Industrial and Commercial Electronics

Commercial and industrial electronics recycling for control, automation and testing equipment.

Control panels, programmable logic controllers, motor drives, automation equipment, test equipment, measurement devices and industrial computers.

10

Security Equipment

Secure recovery and recycling for surveillance and access-control electronics.

Security cameras, DVR and NVR systems, access-control equipment, alarm panels, card readers and intercom systems.

11

Consumer Electronics

Recycling for a broad range of household and personal electronic devices.

Gaming consoles, controllers, home theatre equipment, digital media players, radios, GPS devices, calculators and electronic toys.

12

Cables and Accessories

Cable recycling and recovery for electronics accessories and peripheral equipment.

Power cords, charging cables, network cables, adapters, chargers, power bars, computer accessories and electronic peripherals.

13

Circuit Boards and PCBs

Printed circuit board recycling and commodity recovery for separated boards and electronic assemblies.

Circuit boards, printed circuit boards (PCBs), populated boards, server boards, telecom boards, computer boards and other electronic assemblies containing recoverable components.

Items outside our typical scope

Large household appliances are not typically accepted.

CPER focuses on electronics, IT assets, components, circuit boards, cables and equipment containing electronic controls.

We do not typically accept:

Large household appliances such as refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ovens, dishwashers, washing machines and dryers.

Unsure whether an item qualifies? Send us the equipment type, quantity and photographs so we can review it before pickup.

Common recycling questions

Electronics acceptance and pickup information

Do you recycle computers and IT equipment?

Yes. We accept desktops, laptops, MacBooks, workstations, servers, monitors, storage equipment, networking equipment, peripherals and many computer components.

Do you accept circuit boards, PCBs and cables?

Yes. We accept printed circuit boards, populated boards, server and telecom boards, power cords, network cables, chargers, adapters and many related electronic accessories.

Can medical or laboratory electronics be recycled?

Many types can be reviewed, but equipment must satisfy regulatory and contamination requirements and may require documentation or confirmation of decontamination.

Have computers, IT equipment or electronics ready for recycling?

Tell us what you have, where it is located and the approximate quantity. We will review the equipment and confirm pickup or delivery options.
