Electronics accepted for recycling

If it has a circuit board or a cable, ask us about it.

CPER accepts a broad range of computers, IT equipment, mobile devices, networking gear, office electronics, circuit boards, cables and specialized electronics.

Ask About Your Equipment
01

Computers and IT Equipment

Desktop computers, laptops, workstations, servers, thin clients, monitors, docking stations, keyboards, mice and external drives.

02

Mobile Devices

Smartphones, cellphones, tablets, e-readers, smartwatches, hotspots and handheld devices.

03

Networking and Telecom

Routers, switches, modems, firewalls, wireless access points, VoIP phones, PBX systems and network appliances.

04

Data-Centre Equipment

Servers, storage arrays, SAN/NAS systems, racks, power distribution units, backup equipment and UPS systems.

05

Computer Components

Processors, RAM, drives, motherboards, graphics cards, power supplies, circuit boards, fans and expansion cards.

06

Office Electronics

Printers, scanners, photocopiers, fax machines, label printers, barcode scanners, POS systems and payment terminals.

07

Audio and Video Equipment

Televisions, projectors, digital signage, speakers, amplifiers, receivers, cameras and video equipment.

08

Medical and Laboratory Electronics

Patient monitors, diagnostic equipment, optical and optometry equipment, laboratory instruments and testing devices, subject to decontamination and acceptance requirements.

09

Industrial and Commercial Electronics

Control panels, PLCs, motor drives, automation equipment, test equipment, measurement devices and industrial computers.

10

Security Equipment

Security cameras, DVR/NVR systems, access-control equipment, alarm panels, card readers and intercom systems.

11

Consumer Electronics

Gaming consoles, controllers, home theatre equipment, digital media players, radios, GPS devices and electronic toys.

12

Cables and Accessories

Power cords, charging cables, network cables, adapters, chargers, power bars and electronic peripherals.

13

Circuit Boards and PCBs

Printed circuit boards, populated boards, server boards, telecom boards, computer boards and related electronic assemblies.

A wide range of electronics

From complete systems to individual components.

Electronics come in all shapes and sizes. We regularly handle complete computers and servers, but also circuit boards, cables, components, networking gear and specialized electronic equipment.

If you are not sure whether something fits, send us a photo or model number. We would rather take a quick look than have you guess.

Ask About Your Equipment
Outside our usual scope

Large household appliances are not typically accepted.

CPER focuses on electronics, IT assets, components, circuit boards, cables and equipment containing electronic controls.

We do not typically accept refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ovens, dishwashers, washing machines or dryers. If you are unsure, send us a photo before loading it up.
Start here

Tell us what you have

You do not need a perfect inventory. A rough count, a few model numbers and some photos are usually enough to start. In our service areas, qualifying commercial loads can be collected at no charge, with no membership fee required.
