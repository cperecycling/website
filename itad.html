IT asset disposition

Retiring a lot of technology? Keep the process simple.

CPER helps organizations move retired IT equipment out of the building, secure the data, identify remaining value and document where assets went.

Discuss an ITAD ProjectRecover Equipment Value
Built for real-world IT refreshes

From pickup through final disposition.

IT asset disposition does not need to become another complicated project. Share your inventory, locations and reporting requirements. We can structure the rest around your timeline.

1
Collection and chain of custody

Pickup coordination for offices, warehouses, data rooms and multi-location projects.

2
Inventory and asset audit

Make, model and serial-number capture can be included when needed.

3
Data sanitization

Storage devices are securely erased or physically destroyed based on project requirements.

4
Reuse and value recovery

Reusable devices can be tested, graded, refurbished, sold or donated rather than prematurely recycled.

5
Final reporting

Disposition records and certificates are available for organizations that require documentation.

Where we can help.

Laptop and desktop replacement

Servers, storage and networking

Cleanout

Warehouse and facility projects

Handle mixed electronics and accumulated IT equipment during moves, closures and storage cleanouts.

ESG and sustainability reporting

Turn disposition records into useful sustainability data.

For projects where reporting matters, CPER can provide information that helps support ESG and sustainability reporting, including serialized asset records, reuse outcomes, data-destruction documentation and end-of-life disposition details.

Reporting can be tailored to the project so your team receives practical documentation without creating unnecessary administrative work.

Reporting

Useful for internal ESG programs

Project records can help support electronics diversion, reuse, data-security and disposition reporting for internal sustainability programs, customer requirements or corporate reporting.
