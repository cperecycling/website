Secure data destruction

Your equipment can leave. Your data shouldn’t.

CPER handles data-bearing devices using NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 aligned Clear, Purge or Destroy methods, depending on the media, security requirement and final disposition.

Practical experience matters. CPER brings 20+ Years of Industry Experience to projects involving data-bearing equipment, reuse and end-of-life processing. Free commercial pickup is available for qualifying loads, with no membership fee required.

Choose the right level of destruction

Secure handling that fits the equipment and the risk.

Not every drive needs to be shredded. Some devices can be securely sanitized and reused; others should be physically destroyed. We can help determine the appropriate method for your project.

Questions

Data destruction FAQ

Can you provide a certificate of data destruction?

Yes. Certificates and serialized reporting are available for projects that require them. Tell us what documentation your organization needs when booking the service.

Can working computers be wiped instead of destroyed?

Often, yes. When secure sanitization is appropriate, keeping a working device intact can preserve reuse or resale value while protecting the previous owner’s information.

Do you follow NIST SP 800-88?

CPER’s data sanitization process is aligned with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 guidance, using Clear, Purge or Destroy methods as appropriate.
