CPER CONTACT FORM - ONE-TIME ACTIVATION

The site forms submit directly to info@cperecycling.ca through FormSubmit.

After uploading the website:
1. Visit https://cperecycling.ca/contact.html
2. Submit a test form.
3. Open the activation email sent by FormSubmit to info@cperecycling.ca.
4. Click the activation link.
5. Submit another test form and verify that it arrives.

All site forms require name, email, phone number, pickup/equipment address, city and equipment details.
