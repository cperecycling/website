Technology with a second life

Good equipment can do more than sit in storage.

CPER is a registered non-profit built around a simple idea: keep useful technology useful for as long as practical, then recycle it responsibly when it reaches the end of its life.

Donate Retired EquipmentRequest Refurbished Equipment
Reuse before recycling

A computer can be retired from one job and still be useful for another.

Businesses often replace technology because of refresh cycles, standardization or changing software requirements-not because every device has stopped working.

When practical, CPER evaluates working equipment for refurbishment, resale, parts recovery or donation. This keeps usable devices in service longer and reduces the amount of material that needs to be processed as e-waste.

CPER is a registered non-profit organization, but it is not a registered charity and does not issue charitable tax receipts.
How the model works

Reuse supports the mission.

Some donated or collected equipment may be refurbished for community use. Other reusable equipment may be resold, with proceeds helping fund CPER’s non-profit operations and community initiatives.

For charities & non-profits

Need refurbished equipment?

If your registered charity or non-profit organization could make use of refurbished computers or other electronics, contact CPER and tell us what you need.

Availability changes, so we cannot promise specific models or quantities. We can review current inventory and let you know whether there may be a fit.

Easy to participate

Reuse should be simple for your organization.

For qualifying commercial loads, CPER can arrange free pickup in our service areas. There is no membership fee or annual contract required to take part in the program.
