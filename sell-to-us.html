Turn surplus equipment into value

A responsible buyback option for reusable electronics and IT assets.

Maximize the value of your retired technology. We offer competitive returns for select reusable equipment, securely erase data from storage devices, and responsibly manage your retired IT assets. Proceeds from refurbishment and resale help support our non-profit mission and community initiatives.

If you prefer, we can also arrange for the value recovered from your equipment to be directed to a charity of your choice.

Many electronics recycling and ITAD companies may charge fees to recycle retired equipment. CPER provides free commercial electronics recycling pickups and may pay for select equipment with recoverable value.

Equipment valuation

What determines the value of retired technology?

Not every electronic item has resale value. Quotes are based on the expected value remaining after testing, handling, data processing, refurbishment, logistics and resale costs.

Secure data management

Data-bearing devices can be securely sanitized or physically destroyed as required. Serialized reporting and certificates are available upon request.

Model and Specifications

Processor generation, memory, storage, configuration and manufacturer affect market demand.

Condition and Completeness

Working condition, cosmetic quality, passwords, chargers and missing components can affect value.

Quantity and Consistency

Larger, uniform lots are often more efficient to test, process and remarket.

Current Market Demand

Technology values change based on age, supply, resale demand and commodity markets.

Frequently asked questions

Selling electronics and IT equipment to CPER

What information should I send for a quote?

Include equipment types, model numbers, quantities, specifications, condition, location and clear photographs or an inventory file.

Do you purchase every electronic item?

No. Purchasing is limited to eligible equipment with sufficient reuse, resale, component or commodity value. Other electronics may still qualify for recycling.

Can you collect equipment from our location?

Pickup may be available based on location, volume, equipment type and route availability. We will confirm logistics during the evaluation.

Request a quote for your surplus electronics and IT equipment.

Send an inventory, model numbers, approximate quantities, condition details and photographs. We will review the equipment and confirm whether it qualifies for purchase or value recovery.
